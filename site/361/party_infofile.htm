1.From the West
1.1 From Holland, England
Follow the A! to the german Border (near of Henglo) Then follow the A30 (E30) until you reach the A2 (E30). Follow this lane to Hannover, Braunschweig, Magdeburg. Hear you take the E49 until you reach the A9 (E39/E51). Hear you drive in direction of Gera. Follow this lane until you reach the A72 (E441). Drive in direction of Zeickau / Dresden. Take the exit Reichenbach, and follow signs to Lengenfeld and there the hopefully existing signs to the party place.

1.2 From the South of France
Travel to Frankfurt via the A5 (E35). Then drive in direction of Giessen / Kassel until you reach the A4 (E40). Follow the lane until you reach the A9 (Ee39/E51). Then drive in direction of Plauen / Hof. Follow this lane until you reach the A72 (E441). Drive in direction of Zeickau / Dresden. Take the exit Reichenbach, and follow signs to Lengenfeld and there the hopefully existing signs to the party place.
