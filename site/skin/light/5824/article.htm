As you probably know, besides of (quite slowly) writing Freax, I also have other hobbies. Among them, running Hungary's most popular blogsite, along with my t-shirt shop. (It's http://blog.tomcatpolo.hu for all you willing to learn Hungarian.) It has a daily updated section titled 'Droidzone' where I collect stories of stupid people. This thing attracts more than 2000 unique visitors a day.

Recently I've collected some of these stories into a book, and it's out already. No, it's not English, but probably someday I'll translate it. What the heck has it got to do with Freax? Well, the income generated by this little book will provide most of the financial ground for the Big Scene Book. If everything goes well - and according to my sales reports, it does - we're going to sell 2000 copies during the Christmas season and after.

But as I look at the figures, although we're closer to meet the expenses, we're still not there. We still need some 1 million forints, that is 4000 Euros. I have an idea how to gather it, but it needs your help. The entire thing comes down to a simple question: Would you, guys, buy Freax before it's even printed? We'd set up a website where you can order it by credit card. If around 120 people pre-order the book, we're set, and Freax will be printed. 120 is not much. If we fail to collect this amount in, let's say, 2 months from the site launched, everybody will have his or her money sent back.

Would you preorder and help the project to finally reach its end? Please fill my little poll to answer this question:

http://photo.whois.hu/szavazas/poll.php

(Thanks to Athina for assembling it.)

In the forthcoming days I'll finish translating the Amiga parts, and finalize the entire first volume. After Dan Wright finishes proofreading - he does a very good job anyway - we'll prepare it for printing. If we can collect the money required, we'll press the big red button, and the book enters our reality. Someday soon.
