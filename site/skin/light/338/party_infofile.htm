.introduction

we would like to invite you to a demoscene party which
will take place on 3-4 august nearby gliwice in slask. our aim is
to organize this party in a best possible way. we are going to
get back to the roots of computer productions and make sure this
party has its exceptional atmosphere. the party place is situated
in a small town called 'miedary':). the way you can get there is
not very complicated, you can find information about that further
on in this invitation. we have tried to choose the date of the
party so that it does not collide with other parties.

.from our side

we will try to provide you with best conditions. we have
rented a high quality hall and quite decent sound equipment.
there is a room for about 150 people in fine - according to us -
conditions. however, if it turned out that there are more people
who would like to come (which we would find a nice surprise) we
would do our best to make sure there is enough room for everybody
;) that is why it is essential to register on our www site if you
want to come. we will do anything to make the memories of this
party be as pleasant as possible .

.things we expect from you

first of all we expect high involvement from both sides.
the party is for you and it is up to you how it will look like. we
would appreciate high level productions and cultured way of
behaviour - that is the way we perceive a perfect party. we are
aware of the fact that this may not be easy but we believe that
with a bit of goodwill we can achieve this...

.realities

we are sorry to write that but there are people at the
demoscene who do not understand its sense. they are often not able
to appreciate our work. we are talking especially about those
people who treat parties as another opportunity to drink. that is
why we have decided to make this party an invite-only party. we
realize that some people may not like this idea, but please
do understand that it is just for our own good. anyway, anyone who
has come across demoscene's productions and has signed up for the
party on the abstract2oo1 official web site can take part in this
event. the estimated pass-tickets price is 20 pln. ladies and
guests from abroad are exempt from this charge.

.destination: party place

there are two ways of getting to the party place. the
easiest way is to take the direct bus from katowice to tarnowskie
gory (bus line 820) and then take another bus (line 180) from the
bus station in tarnowskie gory and get out at a bus stop called
'miedary posesja 17' which is situated next to the party place (we
will try to mark it somehow :)) it is the most convenient solution
for people outside slask. in any case, there are lots of trains to
katowice from all over poland... but if going to katowice does not
suit somebody, you can always go to gliwice by train and then take
the bus (line 80 that goes from piwna street ->see map on our www)
and get out at a bus stop called 'zbroslawice kosciol' (it takes
about 30-40 minutes to get there). there are some characteristic
signs nearby the place you have to get out at. there is a joint
called 'stodola' on the left, and on the right there are: a wall
with an inscription 'tu jest zly pies' on it (or something like
that ;), a stud farm and in front of the bus stop there is a radio
tower in the centre of this charming place. :) then you take the
line 191 bus (its bus stop is on the opposite side of the street)
you take your way to the 'miedary szkola' bus stop. there is a
military unit just in front of it on the left and a graveyard on
the right. =) after getting out of the bus you should see primary
school building (there is some hall being built next to it) party
place is approx. 200 meters from this place. from time to time we
will try to go out to the bus stop and check if some of you got
lost. you can also search for any information concerning your
arrival on polish state railways' and buses' web sites: www.pkp.pl
www.kzkgop.com.pl, http://hevelius.cnt.pl/~mzkp

.specification

- if someone wants to present a product on the party it is not
essential that he/she be on the party,
- files compressed in *.zip or *.rar formats will only be accepted
- every production must be supplemented with an information file
(author, title, kind of competition),
- as we want to make a just selection from among pieces of music
the deadline for it will be on a couple of days before the party
- maximum 2 works of one author in each compo can be presented,
- works that have previously been released on other parties cannot
take part in competitions,
- compo machine planned configuration:
amd duron 750 mhz, 256 mb ram, geforce 2mx, sblive!, sb16/awe64
- platforms : win 9x/dos 7.0

.competitions

- demo compo : permission for opengl 1.2, dx 8.0, vesa 3.0,
sound has to work on sound blaster,
maximum zip compressed files 10mb,
unlimited duration time, but keep to a common
sense ;),
please place any possible comments in an info file
for organizers (e.g. "4org.txt")

- intro64kb : permission for opengl 1.2, dx 8.0, vesa 3.0,
sound has to work on sound blaster,
maximum size 64kb (65536 bytes),
the whole intro has to be closed in one executable
file,
permission for using exterior extenders (it has to
be enclosed in a given production),
in case of win32 all dll files available in
standard system can be used,
duration time: up to 5 minutes (when justified may
last longer),
please place any possible comments in an info file
for organizers (e.g. "4org.txt")

- intro4kb : permission for opengl 1.2, dx 8.0, vesa 3.0,
works with sound have to work on sound blaster,
maximum size 4kb (4096 bytes),
the whole intro has to be closed in one executable
file,
permission for using exterior extenders (it has to
be enclosed in a given production),
in case of win32 all dll files available in
standard system can be used,
duration time: up to 3 minutes (when justified may
last longer),
please place any possible comments in an info file
for organizers (e.g. "4org.txt")

- 24bpp gfx : resolutions permitted - 640x480, 800x600, 1024x768,
stages of the production process required (max.5),
evident scans will not pass through the selection,
formats - bmp, png, tga, lmb, gif, iff

- image
processing : resolutions permitted - 640x480, 800x600, 1024x768,
self-made photos & rendered 3d objects can be used,
this is NOT a crazy compo,
formats - bmp, png, tga, jpg

- 8bpp gfx : resolutions permitted - 320x200, 320x240,
stages of the production process required (max.5),
evident scans will not pass through the selection,
formats - bmp, png, tga, lmb, gif, iff

- ray gfx : resolutions permitted - 640x480, 800x600, 1024x768,
using someone else's objects prohibited,
changing rays using 2d graphics programmes not
allowed (this does not concern signatures/captions)
if you use 'Poser' programme you have to mention
that in a txt file,
objects must be delivered,
formats - bmp, png, tga, jpg

- vhs compo : productions on cdr/cdrw will only be accepted,
permission for divX,
this is NOT a crazy compo,
duration time: up to 10 minutes (when justified
may last longer)

- mp3 alternative music compo:
this competition concerns every classical demoscene productions
like guitar ballads etc. it means that it applies to
arrangements in which mainly the melody is emphasised.

up to 5 minutes of each mp3 will be presented
(when justified may be more),
minimum constant bitrate: 96kbit,
permission for vbr, of course,
the selection will be carried out before the party
(no more than 10 works will pass through the
selection), all mp3s will be presented using the
latest winamp version

- mp3 progressive music compo:
this competition type refers to progressive productions (as the
name suggests). it often goes far beyond popular music. it is
not only a well comprehended electronic production & ambitious
sort of techno but progressive rock, metal or even acid-jazz
as well. anyway, if you are not sure how to classify your work
the selection will do that for you.

up to 5 minutes of each mp3 will be presented
(when justified may be more),
minimum constant bitrate: 96kbit,
permission for vbr, of course,
the selection will be carried out before the party
(no more than 10 works will pass through the
selection), all mp3s will be presented using the
latest winamp version

- multichannel music compo (also further divided into alternative
and progressive ones):

up to 5 minutes of each production will be
presented (when justified may be more),
formats - xm, it, s3m, mod,
the selection will be carried out before the party
(no more than 10 works will pass through the
selection), all modules will be presented using
the latest modplug or player version (according to
the author's wishes, but it cannot be a tracker!)

- chip music : up to 3 minutes of each production will be
presented (when justified may be more),
formats - xm, it, s3m, mod,
maximum size: 64kb (65536 bytes),
the selection will be carried out before the party
(no more than 10 works will pass through the
selection), all modules will be presented using
the latest modplug or player version (according to
the author's wishes, but it cannot be a tracker!)

- ascii/ansi : unlimited size (but make sure it can be shown at
800x600 resolution),
acidview will be used to present it.


as usual, there will be many imaginative crazy-compos between the
'serious' ones ;)

.ending up
