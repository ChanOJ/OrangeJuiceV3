  AC=ross Competition Survey ..68 clicks..
This is a survey among Commodore 64 sceners to see if enough people would participate in the Across competition.
Here’s the big deal: In association with communities of other 8-bit platforms, we in XeNTaX would invite Commodore 64 sceners to create demos for non-Commodore platforms.
The AC=ross competition is aimed at those Commodore c64 sceners that can show off their coding skills on other platforms, such as MSX, ZX Spectrum, Amstrad etc., but will always feature one platform per competition. More details and the poll can be found at the XeNTaX forum (click the link).
