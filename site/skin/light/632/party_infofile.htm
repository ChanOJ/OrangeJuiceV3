Information about Kazan:

The place of party is Kazan city. Kazan is the capital of the Republic of Tatarstan(russian state
territory populated by tatar nation). The territory of Kazan City stands at 425 km2
populated by about 1,200,000 people. Distance from Moscow 700km. Trip time 10-12 hours(one night).
Round trip(Moscow-Kazan) ticket price in comfortable train about 40 euro(600 russian rubles).
