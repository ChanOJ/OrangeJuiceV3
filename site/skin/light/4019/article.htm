Because the French scene is slowing down, and because there is less and less party in France, we organize: the CRAU.02. 1(day of) 2, 3 , November 2002 in St Martin of crau close to Arles (France 13) www.crauparty.fr.st This party is resolutely directed oldschool, the objective is simple: If we ara less and less numerous in the scene it is that there are no suffisament new sceners and that we have less and less sharing spirit around the simple pleasures of the demoscene. All the components of the scene will be joined together, the amigaists and atarists, the cpcists and the oricians, macintochists and pcists while passing by all the other possible scenes. We hope to count on the mobilization of the scene because without this mobilization, there will be no more party in general and more CRAU in particular. There will be the usual contests but also full with others compos odd where people will be able to take part. Moreover you do not wait at super price or things like that, it is at all the goal, I ensure you that you will have the prices more the mild nutters whom a party gave. We don't want to revolutionize the scene, on the other hand with regard to the votes, for example, we will try out a new system to fight against the ' vote for friends' which penalizes the artists a little strokes about it but it will not be the only ' innovation '. We will try to propose throughout the party of the small sessions of initiation on tricks a little ' space' as the coding on old machines such as Amiga, the atari or the oric, we hope that these sessions will be made in the 3 great major disciplines in the event the code, the graph and the zic. The objective it is also that those which come with the CRAU set out again while having learned or divided some things in order to advance the movement. As you will see it on Ojuice or others, some groups and not of least, support us already and will be present with compos, I hope that you will make party of it. Save Our Scene PS: the Web site came very late, we decided that all the world would pay the price of reservation.
