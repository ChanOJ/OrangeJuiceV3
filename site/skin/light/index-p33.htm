  Makke's Debut Album "It's Binary, Baby!" ..212 clicks..
September 12th is the release date for Makke's debut album "It's Binary, Baby!" The album is comprised of remixes of classic game tunes from the Commodore 64 as well as original material.
It's also the launch date for the Back In Time Live DVD set, with live performances from Stuck In D80s, Press Play On Tape, Visa Röster, The C64mafia, and a piano performance by none other than Rob Hubbard himself. Footage for the DVD was done at Back In Time Brighton in 2003, and Back In Time London 2004.
