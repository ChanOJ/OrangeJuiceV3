I started out on the C64 in 1986 in a very small Australian group called The Excellence Crew - I was known as Ziggy or Z and we made a few funny little demos. Then I joined the Israeli group called The Force and also the French group called Atom and coded some demos for them until I left the scene in 1994.

I later joined a PC group called Paranormal in 1996 and was active until it broke up in 1997.

Then I started Pixolut, which was an idea I had a couple of years ago as a place to experiment with codeing and music ideas. As time has gone on I have worked on music and DJing a lot - but never forgotten my C64 heritage. I did stupid things like web based C64 intro remakes as well as music remixing and DJing sessions.

Earlier this year I opened an HVSC mirror on Pixolut and I have been in touch with some of the old crew... So the new demo was born.

I would like to make Pixolut an active C64 demo and music group - but doing it on my own is tough. Thats why I am looking for guys who are interested in the 64 to help out.

It doesn't need to be a big deal or full time - I have realistic expectations of what people can do in their spare time - I am developing and deploying global infrastructures for multinationals when I am not codeing 64 demos, so I understand if you want to take your time doing things.
I just think it would be good to have some friends contributing and keeping the creative juices flowing for those times we need inspiration.
