  DevLib 1.0 released ..198 clicks..
DevLib is an object-oriented framework written in pure C++. It is designed to make demos easier and more intuitive to write. Consequences are a complete abstraction of resources management (fonts, images, 3D meshes, files, zip-archives, sounds..) and rendering operations through 3D hardware.
This represents a whole year of programming on my free-time.
I hope DevLib will be spreaded all around the world! ;)
