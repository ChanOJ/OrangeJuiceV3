An extensive essay on the demoscene and its art, written in March 2002 as part of a bigger study for the University of Catania, Italy, and translated in English a few months ago.
Aim of this report is to prove that the demoscene (and its creations) is a true form of art, far brither than other "official" and more famous ones. A large part of the essay is devoted to the Spaceballs, Bomb!, Kolor, the Music of the Scene, Blasphemy, Elitegroup, Farbrausch and many other groups.
