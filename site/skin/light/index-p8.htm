  Plopbox, the online oldschool jukebox ..244 clicks..
Plopbox is something between an oldschool music archive and a web radio. You can browse through a huge database of tunes and play them directly from your browser without the need of any software. The java applet plays XM/S3M/AHX/FC/YM/MOD and SID is coming. Members can have access to some powerful playlist which allow them to use Plopbox as a personalized web radio, and also submit some quality music and vote.
