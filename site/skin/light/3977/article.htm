It is called « State of the Art ». It will be held on December 13-14-15 2002 in Tourcoing (near Lille, France) in a unique place : Le Fresnoy (http://www.le-fresnoy.tm.fr), a national Studio of Contemporary Arts and a wonderful building which used to be a Wrestling arena ! A mythical place for an oldschool coding-party. We will go back in time to the early days of Atari-Amiga demo in order not to forget where this passion comes from and where it goes. 1) State of the Art will be hosted by the Pixel Festival, a festival dedicated to the “Digital Culture” and organized by L’Arc Numérique, a group of digital companies from Lille and around, co-organised by Le Fresnoy and L’Aéronef. Many professionals from the video games, SFX or sound industries will attend the event and present their techniques along a series of conferences (English translation provided). Special guests of renown in their field of expertise, code, gfx, music are expected. Of course State of the Art will provide the usual competitions with quality prizes. We are aiming at getting demo-makers and professionals to meet each other in the oldshool spirit. 250 people are expected to attend this digital week-end. 2) Pixel Festival will also host the Vector Lounge (www.vectorlounge.com), an international meeting of web-designers. This event which has taken place in Amsterdam, Paris, Singapore will gather 40 graphic designers of world repute. Vector Lounge and State of the Art will meet at L’aéronef for a wild party. 3) A series of conferences (English translation provided) from some of the big shots of the Digital Culture (developers, musicians or graphic designers) and scientists, academics and officials will be held during the festival. So don’t forget : “State of the Art, 13-14-15 December, Tourcoing, France. A demo-party we will recount our grand-children. Official website on-line soon. kRafton / nOmad
