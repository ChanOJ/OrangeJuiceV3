  Famicompo started ..90 clicks..
To celebrate 20th birthday of Nintendo Family Computer (also known as Nintendo Entertainment System), the first online music competiton for NES 2A03 sound unit - Famicompo - started. The deadline is June 15th.
Nowadays NES music scene is getting interesting day by day. There're already two tracker programs which allows you to create NES tune: NerdTracker II [DOS] and CheeseTracker [Linux]. And if you want, you can also use another program called MCK featuring MML style: sort of text based music edit system which was the most popular method for 8-bit/16-bit music in Japan.
