amiga / pc / 8bit party:
Chaos Constructions 2005 demo party (CC5) is going to take place on 20th and 21st of August. Partyplace is in concert hall of Leningrad's Youth Palace (LDM), St.Petersburg, Russia. Approximately 15 minutes by walk from subway stations Petrogradskaya, Chkalovskaya, Chernaya Rechka.

Following competitions and platformes will be present:

PC/Amiga: 16mb Demo, 64k Intro, 4k Intro, Graphics (Rendered, Handdrawn, Freestyle, Ascii), Music (Multichannel, Mp3, Chip Tune);
8 bit: 640kb Demo, 4kb Intro, 512b Intro, 640kb Game. Additionaly, for ZX Spectrum only: Graphics, AY-music;
Mobile/Handheld: 1mb Demo, 64kb Intro, 1mb Game;
Misc: Wild/Animation, Flash 4mb Demo, 8mb Game;

for more info see website.
