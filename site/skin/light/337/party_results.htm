Ok, here are some results from the real session 3 party,
held in Eisenhuettenstadt/Germany.
About 100 people showed up (only about 15 sceners).

Fast Competition
1. ??? by mKerstin (28)
2. Neon-Limo by mados+Tomaes (16)
3. 96byte by ??? (13)
4. Fassbrause verschieben in RGB by eMKay 84 (11)
5. RGB, verschieben, Fassbrause by ManiacME (10)
6. Mr.Blue by Sereal (5)
(The most interesting compo. At least the first
2 entries are worth watching :))

Game Competition
1. Sugar Cube hunter by Tomaes (36)
2. MS-Game Shooter by MadMan (21)
(Cool, MadMan's fake-entry almost won :))

512k-Intro Competition
1. Planquadrat-B by TAP (36)
(We only "won" because the faque demo was too big
in size, and so, it was entered in the wild-compo, see below.)

Sound Competition
1. Instabil by Tomaes/TAP/NSE (21)
2. Wolken by Zippozipp/Beta-CC (18)
3. GUF by Hardcore Junkie (16)
4. breakin by Zippozipp/Beta-CC (12)
5. sphere by Zippozipp/Beta-CC (8)
(The only tune which was not pure techno won :))

Graphics Competition
1. Forestbook by Lynx Vulgaris (46)
2. Passenger by Ragnarok/Faque/Metalvotze (25)
3. Digital by Tomaes (23)
4. Bridge2 by CeGan (8)
5. Lea by Jack C./Faque (6)
(At least 2 old pics in the compo. But at least no scan fake :))

Wild Competition
1. True Simple by Jack C./Faque (31)
2. The Naked Yakll by GPF/Beta-CC (24)
3. Puzzle by mKerstin (17)

("True Simple" was a rather cool old-skool demo,
which was simply too big for the 512k-intro compo.

has the balls to release it to the public :))

Surprise Compo
1. MadMan/TAP
2. mKerstin
3. Zippozipp/Beta-CC
(The task was to "repair" a destroyed keyboard.)
