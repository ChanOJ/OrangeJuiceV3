Some of you probably remember the demoscene radio program in June 2001 on the Berlin based radio station 'Fritz' (Chaosradio #62). The idea is to establish now a regular demoscene program on the station 'Offener Kanal Berlin', a public non-commercial station where everyone can make its own program (maybe you know such stations from your own town). Until now this station was only receivable by people with Cable TV/Radio in Berlin, but starting from the 14th September the station can be received by everyone in Berlin with a normal radio (means that we can reach 3.5 million people in theory ;-)). Additionally there is also an OGG Vorbis livestream available.

What would you hear on a demoscene radio? Well, besides 100% scene music (also a good chance to reach a bigger audience with your music) it's probably a good idea to talk about new demos, past and upcoming parties or other important things and to make interviews with sceners. Maybe also a 'newbie corner' to introduce the demoscene would be a nice idea. The program would be live and probably max. 60 minutes long (limit from the radio station). Actually I have no idea how often such a program should be made (every month, every two weeks, every week?), depends of course also on the time and motivation of the involved persons.

What's needed? Not *that* much, for playing the music there is atleast a mp3 player needed (hw mp3 or mp3/ogg capable cd player, maybe a laptop or notebook). The interested persons should be from Berlin. Atleast one person must contact the radio station in advance to learn how to use the technic of the radio studio (shouldn't be that complicated, I have made radio in the late 80s and early 90s on the very same station). For the law there must be one person fully responsible for the program (Sendeverantwortlicher).

What's forbidden? Due to the nature of the radio station (the use of the station is completly free) it's forbidden to gain money with the program or to make commercial advertisements. To avoid payments to the record industry the music in the program should be completly free from third party rights (=only your own work, no rips from commercial music).

When will it start? If there are interested persons (I can't and won't make it as a solo project) then the first program can be aired maybe in October. But it's up to you if the idea will come to life or is 'born dead'.
