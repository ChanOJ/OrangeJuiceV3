Network is back in full style with a craking new party!!!

BUT realising that we can't get hold of a big party place,
we decided to gather some people from spain and portugal
at ayame's house (she's the main party orgo \o/).

Having place for only 30 people and 6 computers means
we are fully booked for the start so common
compos and restrictions from other parties would be very
out of place... we are allowing remote entries by email!!!

If there are more entries to the compos than at bcnparty101,
madgoblin/spöntz^zona neutra will tatoo ayame onto himself!!!


amiga demo, intro and intro4k compo
-----------------------------------

compo orga: winden of network, windenntw@gmail.com

compo machine is amiga 1200 with workbench 3.1,
blizzard 060/50 (with MMU and FPU) and 64megs of fast mem.

demo must be <= 16 megabytes (16777216 8-bit bytes) unpacked
intro must be <= 96 kbytes ( 98304 8-bit bytes) unpacked
intro4k must be <= 4 kbytes ( 4096 8-bit bytes) unpacked

please note that there is no ppc card, and maybe AHI is older than
you have. you can mail winden anything you need to test before hand,
no spread of course ;)

demos with commercial music, already presented at other parties
or running more than 10 minutes can be disqualified.

accelerated demo, intro and intro4k compos
------------------------------------------

compo orga: ayame of network, ayamenetwork@gmail.com
winden of network, windenntw@gmail.com

two compo machines are available:

- portable pc, more details will follow soon.

- ibook ppc g4/800mhz, 640megs of main ram
and ati 9200 with 32megs of ram.

demo must be <= 16 megabytes (16777216 8-bit bytes) unpacked
intro must be <= 96 kbytes ( 98304 8-bit bytes) unpacked
intro4k must be <= 4 kbytes ( 4096 8-bit bytes) unpacked

if you need to test pc entries, mail ayame for info.
if you need to test mac entries, mail winden for info.
both no spread of course ;)

demos with commercial music, already presented at other parties
or running more than 10 minutes can be disqualified.

ascii compo
-----------

compo orga: winden of network, windenntw@gmail.com
font is topaz 8
resolution is either 640x256 or 640x512 (you ask for it)
max ascii size is 78 columns and 8 screens tall

graphics compos
---------------

compo orga: ayame of network, ayamenetwork@gmail.com

you have 24bit 2d, 24bit 3d and 8bit pixel compo.
all entries will be provided in PNG or IFF format,
maximum size is 640x512 pixels for all compos

music compos
------------

compos are 4channel protracker music, chip music,
multichannel and mp3.

maximum playing time will be 3 minutes.

more details to follow...


fast compos
-----------

fast2d and fast3d are to draw with crayons on paper
while blindfolded (and probably drunken also X).

fast coding will have to make a paper description and then
explain the effect. maximum explanation time will be 5 minutes.

fast music will have some menagerie and sticks and you have
to make music live to the party attendants. maximum playing time
will be 1 minute.


misc compos

-----------------------------

for people attending the party:

- you have to take part in at least one non fast or misc compo
- you have to be drunken (at least a bit ^^)

- you can bring any crap that makes noise
- you can fuck the scene all you want
- if you need to puke, please use the bathroom O:)

- have fun and make friends, scene rules!!! \o/
