-= another opportunity for the sceners to get together and send THE message =-


Party features
----------------------

FREE ENTRANCE FOR EVERYONE :: FREE PARTY T-SHIRTS :: FREE INET ACCESS

The party will have a big screen about 5 meters wide. It will support
resolutions of 320x200 up to 640x480. At reunion2k there was a problem:
the screen was TOO BIG to fit the party hall and thus we had to use
a smaller one. This year we will try to get a party hall with a bigger
height such that an enormous screen can be installed.

The sound system will be of the highest standards in the sound industry
to provide the event with an incredible atmosphere. This year, the
reunion party will be quite loud, so please bring earplugs if you feel
you are sensitive to high sound levels.

Free InterNet access from the party place. (You need to bring your computer
to the party place in order to take advantage of the InterNet.)

Reunion2k benefited of high speed InterNet connection provided by TotalNet
and KappaNET. The rates were as high as 300k/sec so you may expect similar
results at this year's party as well.

==========================================================================
*PLEASE* let us know in advance if you're planning to enjoy the InterNet
at the party place by bringing your computer. It is the *ONLY* way
we can setup computer places before hand and thus insuring smooth
connection to the party network. We STRONGLY recommend bringing a computer
to the party place to FULLY enjoy the demoscene feeling. This way, you
get to download productions at high speeds, you get to take advantage
of the ONLINE voting easily, get to enjoy network games with your friends,
transfer MP3s and do those last minute changes to your productions in case
you participate in any of the events' competition.
===========================================================================

Please let us know @ p*****m@hotmail.com and/or p******m@msoe.edu if you're
planning to bring your computer.

Party T-shirts will be given for free.

Electricity for all. So, you can install your computer without any problems.

There will be a bar that will provide food and drinks. One strategy this
year is to bring a mobile MacDonald's unit to provide food and drinks.

>> sleeping bags <<

You are \encouraged\ to bring sleeping bags to the party place in case
you need to rest during the event. reunion is a 2 days event and the
likelihood of you staying up all the time is pretty small. there are
cases when people don't sleep even 5 minutes during the entire event
but that may not be YOUR case. so please, bring a sleeping bag in order
to have a comfortable way of resting.


** NOTE: reunion2k1 is a NONSTOP event for TWO DAYS. Thus, no break of any
sort takes place and you are encouraged to remain at the party place
during the duration of the event to FULLY enjoy the atmosphere [see
section on bringing sleeping bags.] Of course, it is not a
requirement to spend all the time at reunion2k1. You can get in
at any time [day or night.] However, it is a lot more fun
to enjoy all the event and fully project yourself in the demoscene
world.

Competitions and general rules
------------------------------------

The organizers reserve the right to disqualify any entry that is
not suitable to attend the competitions for some reason.

You or your group may participate with maximum 03 productions
in the democompo and 4k intro compo.


DEMO: || - The maximum size permitted is 10MB [may be changed to a larger one.]
- The maximum amount of base memory requirement has to be 600kb
- Pure animation (precalculated) files are not permitted.
- The filenames have to comply with the ISO-9660 standard
(special characters not permitted, filenames being in
MS-DOS standard [length 8 + 3 characters.])
- The program has to run without any interaction from the user.
- The program has to support Sound Blaster.
- No info saving on hdd is permitted.


4KB-INTRO: - The maximum size permitted is 4096 bytes
- The maximum amount of base memory requirement has to be 610kb
- The filename has to comply with the ISO-9660 standard
(special characters not permitted, filename being in
MS-DOS standard [length 8 + 3 characters.])
- The program has to run without any interaction from the user.
- Music is not allowed. (even if the intro will contain some
sounds, - they won't be heard.)
- No info saving on hdd is permitted.


A maximum of 2 tracks are allowed / participant.

|| A preselection may take place.


MUSIC: - Maximum amount of channels: 64.
- Maximum permitted size is 4 MB
- Maximum permitted time duration is: 4 minutes.
- The format of the music has to be one of the following:
MOD, S3M, XM, IT
- The filename has to comply with the ISO-9660 standard
(special characters not permitted, filename being in
MS-DOS standard [length 8 + 3 characters.])
>> We will be using Impulse Tracker, Modplug or Winamp 2.74+
for playing the music. (Contact the organizers if you want
your music to be played with a specific tracker. (can only
be the one of the players mentioned above.)

|| ANIMATION
- The animation should not exceed 20minutes.
- The animation is *NOT* limited to only 3dstudio [max]. Any
rendering software may be used and any hardware. However,
the animation needs to be in an .avi or mpeg format and should
be playable on a regular PC.
- The soundtrack of the animation (optional) does not have to
be composed by the animator him/herself as long as proper
credit is given to the composer.


3D STUDIO: - Maximum resolution permitted is 640x480.
- Maximum amount of simultaneous colors: 16m
- Executable files are not permitted.
- The formats of the images have to be JPG or TARGA
(the .3ds has to be presented also.)
- You must submit at least three versions of the unfinished
picture that show the process of creating the picture.
- The filename has to comply with the ISO-9660 standard
(special characters not permitted, filename being in
MS-DOS standard [length 8 + 3 characters.])
*Note: We will be _probably_ using SEA to show the art.

WILD COMPETITION:

- Any crazy/funny animation from filming on the streets
to rendered 3d effects. The only "rule" here is to have
something funny/crazy/abnormal. However, please avoid any
extreme violence/pornography in your productions.

- The format should be .AVI or MPEG. However, we are willing
to make the required efforts to play it from a regular VHS
cassette if you have no way of converting your production to
standard animation computer files. If this is the case, please
contact us in advance to make all the arrangements.

WEBDESIGN COMPETITION:

- FLASH/HTML/PHP/etc allowed. The pages w/ all the necessary required
files need to be brought to the party place in order to be presented.
However, the page may be loaded straight from the InterNet. THIS IS NOT
RECOMMENDED since the connection to the particular site may not be as fast
as required OR the site may be down for some reason therefore preventing
the entry from being presented.

- The site may be non-interactive [as a demo] or interactive as a regular
site. For the second case, it is recommended that the author presents the
features of the site by talking to the audience on a microphone. This is
not required, but it is recommended.

- Each page will have a 15-minute time period for presentation.

All the entries to the competitions mentioned above will be collected at
the party place.

- 333+ Mhz machine. More details to come.
