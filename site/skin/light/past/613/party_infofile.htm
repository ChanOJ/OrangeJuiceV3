WELCOME TO BCNPARTY'11
In bcn party there is only room for creative people and persons involved with audiovisual computer art. Keep in mind that bcn party is not a gaming or lan party.


FEATURES
- Party hall can easily host 200 people.
- Computer places limited.
- Bring your machine along or show us your stuff on any platform: PC,AMIGA,MAC,MSX,C64,...
- Scene compos and other surprise competitions.
- There'll be workshops and round table on demoscene related subjects.
- Our traditional floppy disk throwing competition.
- Beamer and some bitchin' sound gear. Available for official competitions as well as all attendants that are willing to show the stuff they've been working on, provided you ask first.
- Electricity for everyone (and then some).
- Every seat will have ONE mains power outlet (bring your own splits and/or adapters), sockets are European standard.
- There will be an internal network, and ONE network cable will be available on every seat.
- There'll be wireless access points. So feel free to bring your wireless network card, if you have one.
- Internet access will be provided, use it wisely.
- Access to showers, steam room and acclimatized swimming pool.
- Quiet sleeping area within the premises, separate from the main party hall.
- Catering service. Generous sandwiches and soft drinks at affordable prices.
