.introduction

Phones, mails, talks with "white collars", a little bit of
lost time and finally we can do it...

Thatås mean

We invite You to the next edition of demonscene party called
"abstract2oo2". Special atmosphere - the spirit of demoscene - we
will enliven at the scene of the Old Theatre, precisely saying in
its ruins. Details are in the part called "where - party places".
This time you will have no problem with getting to the place
because it is in the centre of Gliwice. Deadline is set on
12-13 October. We hope that you will manage to complete your work.
At the moment we have at ours disposal High quality film projector
with big screen, and quite good sound and moody lights. So far
everything goes fine. But things aren't those ones who make the
atmosphere. That's why yours presence is so important for us. We
want your nickname to be placed at the abstract identity card.

If you want to take part in our party we ask you to send us a mail
with your nickname and the name of the group and the information
will you be able to take your computer with you. Please do it only
if you are 90% sure that you will come.

.On our part

Ours intention is to create a suitable conditions to make
a cultural party. That is the most important thing for us. We want
ot prove, that demoscene party can have different assumptions then
others modern parties. We hope that in a small piece it was
realized at the previous edition. We think that it is very
creative. We are indignant at unconstructive critique and lack of
tolerance and that is what we want to avoid at our party

.What we expect from you

Our expects from you didn't change. We expect you commitment
in creating the party. High level of yours productions and
the unforgettable atmosphere of the party would be a great
pleasure for Us. We know that it won't be easy, but together we
can make one of the most interesting demoscene parties that took
place. But the most important is your presence.

.realities

The thing which helps us in organizing is your confirmation
of arrival. But do it only if you're 98% sure that you attend the
party. We need it to estimate how many chairs, tables or computer
places prepare at the party place or to provide enuff food ( we're
going to prepare a place where you can buy some cheap food and
consume it etc. ). If you want to take some rest at the party
place you need to supply yourself with a sleeping-bag... We can
guarantee only some cardboards ;) Entry fee will be more or less
20 - 30 pln, depends on number of ppl registrated at our site.
Girls will enter for free J . Guests from ouside Poland will pay
only 1% of actual fee. If you take a computer (and monitor too)
with yourself, we' ll grant you the access ;) for only 10pln. Do
not forget about some extension rod. We wouldn't like you to treat
our party as another occasion to drink only, that's not the point
we're organizing the party for. Why does demoscene party associate
with "mass becoming sober" ? Other things like drugs are regulated
by polish law. Gliwice, as any other bigger polish city is not too
safe place, so we stronly advise not to leave the party place
alone after sunset. There's a free cd with party stuff included in
the entry fee. We think that you'll be nicely suprised by our
bigscreen, which dimensions are 6 x 4 meters.

.how to get to us

Tought by experience from last years (hi Acid!:)we won't force
you to sightseeing whole the Silesia. This time we offer you only
10 minutes walk through the Gliwice center. After lving Gliwce
railway station "PKP" you go straight it the way to McDonaldås -
in front of it you will find Old theater ruins ¾ our party place.

As you can see it is not so hard, but if somebody have any
problems we give more details. You can also find maps and photos
of our city on our site.

After living the railway station you go straight.Than You pass
quite a big square ( on your left you will see taxis and on your
lef you will find PKS station, and naxt to it you will see some
fast foods bars). In front of you you should see Bank of Silesia.
You reach the crossroads with the traffic lights. After walkin on
the other seit of the street to Zwyciestwa Street. You have to go
straight this street passing crossroads with Duboisa, Predry,
Barlickiego street and after 35 second you will reach McDonald. On
the left athe the other site of the street in the alley you will
see Old Theater Ruins.

Ass you can see it is very easy to get to our party so you
shouldynt have any problem.

.where? - party place

The atmosphere of the Old theater ruins is beyond description.
It is hard to close it in any form. There took places many
different cultural parties in this ruins, here performed many
famous polish artists. All of them were talking how unusually this
place is. It is not nice comfortable theatre. It is different
around here - raw, dirty walls, huge space. Ruins have its own
dimension and that causes to do our demosceneparty here. A little
bit of the great atmosphere is shown at the pictures at our
website.
