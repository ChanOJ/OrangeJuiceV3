What is Optihack?

Optihack is Gothenburg’s first large LAN-event. Over 2000 people gathers in the Swedish Fair with the own computers. You socialize, play, compete, program, surf on the web, chas and enjoy the wonderful entertainment offered on the scene. Optihack is open 24 hours a day, Thursday to Sunday noon. In association with Optihack, the first public fair with focus on E-life in Swedish history will be held. Access to the fair costs 6€.

What is E-Life festival?

E-Life festival is the fair which will be held during Optihack. The fair has its focus on the culture around computers and E-sports. In the fair-area a giant funpark will be built. All LAN-participants will have free-access to this area. All info about E-life can be found here.

Where is Optihack?

Optihack will take place in the Swedish fair in the heart of Gothenburg. The premises have an area of 7500 square meters. Café, dressing rooms, showers and toilets are available either in or in connection to the fair.

How much does a ticket cost?

A ticket costs 500 SEK, that is about 54 Euro.

When is Optihack?

Optihack will open the gates att 2.00 P.M on Thursday the 6th of January 2005 and will continue until 12.00 A.M on Sunday the 9th of January 2005. At 10.00 A.M on Sunday the network will be shut down and at 12.00 A.M the power goes off.

Network and Internet access

Every visitor will be provided with a port and a static ip. In our network everybody will have acces to a Internet connection of 1 Gpbs. Zyxel and Extreme Network sponsor us with advanced equipment. To be able to connect your computer to the network you'll need a 10 meter long network cable. If you don't have your own cable we till have cables for sale at Optihack.

Food and beverages

Optihack will have our own cafe open 24/7 with tasty food and all necessities. Everything from baguettes to pasta will be served . There will be a huge sortiment of beverages. More info closer to the event.
