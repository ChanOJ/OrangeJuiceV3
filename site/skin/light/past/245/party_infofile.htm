1.OPENING WORDS
WE NEVER STOP!
Abort97, Abort98, Abort99 and we just keep on going making it better each
year.

Ok, so another Abort is going to happen and again it's going to rule!
Yet this year we have taken our idea further. Our goal is not just to make a
demo party, but to make a happening that will unite not only demo scenners,
but also all other people that find computers fun & challenging to use.

We aim high, but we have confidence that we will make a great event with
great people having fun, interacting, exchanging ideas and knowledge and
most importantly making new friends and meeting old ones.

In this spirit we have decided that beside demo party Abort will also
include Linux festival where people from free & open software community will
show what they do and what they stand for.

We hope we will be able to organize future Aborts (2001...), but this will
only be possible if you show up at the camp & participate in the happening
and competitions!

This is a NON-PROFITABLE event, all the income will be spent for the event
itself.

STAY TUNED FOR MORE DETAILED VERSION OF THIS INVITATION!

2. WHERE and WHEN?

The Abort2000 will be held from 10th (Friday) to 12th November (Sunday) in
Ljubljana/Slovenia in part of Gospodarsko Razstavisce (City fair grounds).

The address:
Gospodarsko Razstavisce,
Dunajska c. 18
Ljubljana
Slovenija

Camp place will open at 10:00 on Friday and close at 12:00 on Sunday.

On the first day there will be an emphasis on Linux festival and on the second
day (Saturday) demo competitions will take place.
Also different kinds of presentations will be available.

Check the section 9. for more information on how to get there!
Stay tuned for further versions of this invitation.

4. ENTRANCE FEES & RESERVATIONS

Everyone that applies in advance trough web will get a free entrance.
Those who will not apply in advance will have to pay a small entrance fee.

Chairs, tables, electricity & network connectivity will be available to
everyone. You have to bring SAFE electricity cables with you, network
equipment is welcomed too.

If you are planning your visit, let us know TODAY! (www.abort2000.org)

We would like to encourage you to bring your computer with you. Computers are
very important part of the party, if you don't have one with you, you won't be
able to show your work to your friends and have other fun during 'free' time.
Don't forget to bring network cards & cables with you!
Also electronic voting will be much easier if you'll have your own computer.

5. COMPETITIONS

There will be some standard competitions and some special ones (surprise!).
Because there is almost no interest in Amiga, Commodore & other compos,
only PC compos will be held. If you have your artwork on any other
platform, you'll have to bring all necessary hardware with you. If there
will be enough interest in any other competition that is not described
below we will make additional compos!

There will also be special Hacking contest. There will be a Linux machine
on the network waiting to be hacked, and its security will be loosened
every now and then until someone hacks it.

Every person can participate with ONE artwork in each competition.
In demo competition every group can have as many entries as it wants.

Entries must not be shown on any other party/competition before Abort.

Anything received after the deadline won't be competing, but it will be
shown. There will be a deadline countdown!

If there won't be enough entries for the competition, they will be shown
/played, but they won't compete!

If there will be too many entries in a competition, organizers will make
a pre-selection to lower the number of entries to reasonable amount. We will
do that the best way we can but nobody is perfect. (preselection is expected
in music competition)

You'll have the opportunity to test your demo TWO TIMES on compo computer. But
don't come just before deadline!

By competing in any of Abort2k competitions you agree that WITHOUT your
written permission the following is true:
Your artwork contributed to Abort2k is free for distribution over all types of
media as long as it hasn't been changed in any way.
Your artwork contributed to Abort2k can be used by LUGOS for promotional use
in sense of Internet, BBSes and CD-release distributions even if a
distribution fee is charged for the CD-release.
Your artwork contributed to Abort2k can be publicly shown at any time
during the event and post-event promotions.
Naturally, the copyright will remain fully yours!

With these rules we will be able to promote the party properly!
Also the organizers have the right to remove any entry from the competition
if they think it is not suitable.



The party PC that will run everything:
Processor: K7 Athlon 1000Mhz
Video card: Matrox G400
RAM: At least 128Mb
Sound card: SoundBlaster AWE 64
Projector: The maximum supported resolution by video projector is 800x600!
Higher resolutions will probably result in an ugly picture!

The configuration is not finalized yet, watch out for further versions of this
invitation text!

Available system configuration will include full VBE 2.0 support!
It will be possible to run Windows and Linux demos too.
Players used the for music compos: latest version of Modplug player and
Impulse Tracker!


* PC DEMO COMPETITION
-maximum size 8388608 bytes
-it should run trough a batch file called START.BAT
-pure animation files not accepted
-maximum base memory requirement is 540Kb
-maximum memory requirement is 64Mb
-if it crashes, it is NOT showed again, but can still compete
-maximum 15 minutes

* PC 64Kb INTRO COMPETITION
-all the rules of demo compo +
-maximum size 65536 bytes
-maximum 5 minutes
-chances for this compo to take place are not good, if there won't be
enough entries, it will be merged with the PC DEMO COMPO

* MULTICHAN MUSIC COMPETITION
-any format that is supported under latest modplug or Impulse Tracker
except MPEG & WAV (pure sound files (non-tracked) wont be accepted)
-maximum 4 minutes playing time, after that it will be faded out
-no remixes allowed

* MP3 MUSIC COMPETITION
-music can be produced in ANY way you wish, no restrictions
-MP3 format, maximum 256kbps bitrate
-maximum 4 minutes playing time, after that it will be faded out
-no remixes allowed

* GRAPHICS COMPETITION
-max. 800x600x16M
-viewer will be .... ?
-accepted formats: PCX, LBM, GIF, BMP, JPG, PNG
-You must add 3 significative steps of making the picture

* PIXELART COMPETITION
-320x200x256 graphics
-accepted formats: PCX, LBM, GIF, BMP, PNG
-You must add 3 significative steps of making the picture!!!
-It must be REAL pixelart, that means no scans, remixes of other pictures
etc...

* 3D/RAYTRACE GRAPHICS COMPETITION
-all rules like for the graphics compo except it has to be made in one of
raytracing programs
-3 steps of constructing must be added! (not different types of shading!)

* ANIMATION & VIDEO COMPETITION
-maximum size is 1 CD (74min)
-maximum rolling time is 10 minutes(if it is longer it will be stopped)
-it has to include computer-made animations, not only recorded video
-must be playable in windows media player(cinepac codec) or in
linux (mpeg2)
-audio is optional, but it would be kinda good to have it:)

* ANSI/ASCII COMPETITION
-max 80x200 ANSI/ASCII
-it has to be made for 80x25 or 80x50 mode
-will be shown with latest ANSIDRAW
-anyone still makes this stuff, please let us know... :)

* WILD THING
-anything allowed
-bring ALL NECESSARY equipment with you (like VCRs, Lightshows,
VR machines, go-go dancers, ...)

* SURPRISE COMPOS
-be prepared for everything :)

* SPECIAL 'HACKING' COMPO
-hack the specified machine that will run Linux
-security will be loosened gradually over time. The one that
first changes the web pages will be the winner

* PARSEK FLASH CONTEST (pfc)
-Precise rules aren't known yet, but everything made in Flash will
probably be accepted

Please include information about every product in product.nfo file!
(that includes author, group, name of the artwork and your e-mail)

6. VOTING SYSTEM

There will be local TCP network organized at the party. It will be connected
to outer world (internet) trough 2Mbps line.
The voting will be done trough local web server that will also collect all
the votes in real time.
You will be able to vote from all machines connected to local network
(yours for example) and from dedicated machines (for those who won't bring
their computer with them).

Every person will get his username and password on the entrance (He'll
have to have a personal ID or some other kind of identification)

10. FOOD, DRINKS & SLEEP
There are restaurants 50m away from the Abort2000 camp, also NON-STOP
fast food is located near the entrance gates.
We will still try to make food and non-alcoholic drinks available all the
time at the camp itself, however we can't guarantee you that.

Alcoholic drinks, drugs & smoking in the camp hall will be strictly forbidden.

If anyone should need anything else gas station with non-stop store is
located 500m away.

There will be space to sleep at the camp hall itself, we hope to manage
to get it a bit separated from the main camp, so there won't be 6000W sound
system just above your head when you'll try to catch some sleep. All you
need are sleeping bags.

Unfortunately showers won't be available.
