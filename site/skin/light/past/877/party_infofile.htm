ZX Spectrum party (KidSoft'2005 Festival).

Version 2.0

-----Date:------------------------------------------------------
The festival takes place on the 24th of December, 2005.

-----Address:---------------------------------------------
Palace of the Children▓s and teenager, Voronezh city,
Children▓s Square; 1 (admission free)

-----Contests:--------------------------------------------------
Everybody can take part in ZX Spectrum platform contest.
(Inspite of their professional level or age.)

-----Pixel Graphics:-------------------------------------------
Only one piece of work is wanted from a person or a creative group.
Image size: standard screen 256X192 pt., 6912 bt. It is forbidden to give
scanned images or images imported from other computers that have been
handmade or partially handmade. Your image must be original. To prove it,
it is advised to have no less than 10 intermediate version of your work.
Otherwise the jury will have to undervalue your image by discounting the
results.

-----Synthesized music: (co-processor AY-3-8910A)-------
Only one piece of work is wanted from a person or a creative group.
Maximum size is 16384 bt. It must coordinate with "Best View" or ZX AMP.
The maximum length of playing is 3 min.

-----Programming:--------------------------------------
Only one piece of work is wanted from a person or a creative group.
Any programm product is possible (game, demo, intro, System programm, etc.)
Erotic or porno contenting programm products is off the contest, as well as ones
showing scenes of violence. Programm products made by Basic language are
also forbidden. The maximum length of demonstration is 10 min.
Attention! The programm must coordinate with Pentagon 128 computer.
The jury will referee using the following criteria:
- professional level
- sound
- graphics
- design
- idea
Each will be given points which form the final result.

-----General rules:----------------------------------------
Only one piece of work is wanted for every category. Those products that
have taken part in any other festivals can not be competitors in this festival.
Filling in the Registration Card is obligatory for all competitors.
If your work is of low quality or out of moral conditions of the festival,
the jury may ask the audience to give it their sympathy prize.
If you can not take part in the festival, you will be given the reasons of
disqualification beforehand. The administration leaves themselves the right
to change the festival rules.

-----Registration Card:-------------------------------------
To be a competitor for any category you have to fill in the Registration
Card and then send it with your contest work to one of the address given
below. Please, be care full and attentive filling in the Registration Card.
This is important for the future diplomas and sending the prizes. It is also
useful for the organization committee.
