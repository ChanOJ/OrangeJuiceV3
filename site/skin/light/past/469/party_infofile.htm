S O L S K O G E N 2 0 0 2
natural scene habitat

___________________________________________________what:

solskogen 2002 is arranged by spaceballs, excess,
kvasigen and ephidrena, currently the four most active
demogroups in norway.

we are dedicated to give you an excellent summer scene
party with sun, cold beer, cool productions and all of
your old and new friends!

____________________________________________________why:


so, why should you come? because this is a 100% sceners
only party, and the only one held this summer in
norway! also; we'll be hosting a multitude of
competitions for all the active platforms; c64, amiga
and pc!

there are of course other reasons; it's cheap, it's
well organized, it's close to the capital of norway
and also to the border to sweden so it's easy to
access for almost everyone.

compos! a multitude of competitions for everyone to
enter: c64 demo, c64 graphics, c64 music, amiga demo,
amiga 64k, pc demo, pc 64k, tracked music, ogg/mp3
music, 24bpp graphics, 8bpp graphics, wild compo,
pr0n leeching compo, disk throwing and of course many
surprise compos and other fun stuff!

also; you can enter in the compos without being at
the party, but we would of course hope that you come
in person and share a beer with us. :)

here are some more key-points to get you in the
mood / inform you better:

* the party will be held in ås, norway. (45 minutes
from oslo, 1 hour from sweden) the last weekend of
june (28-30.06.2002)

* we will be hosting a birthdayparty for spaceballs
and almost all of the old boys will be there!

* rumours has it that the legendary fjortiss
productions will be giving a short live performance..
(sssh!)

* dj's will be playing nice tunes when the sun goes
down.

* there will be a tent-park for sleeping, so bring
your tent, bbq and that summer party-spirit!

* the computers will be set up indoors, so you don't
have to worry about rain. there will ofcourse be a
chance to move your stuff indoors if the weather
doesn't turn out to be on our side.

* large bbq! coal and fire provided by us.

* soccer, basketball, badminton and volleyball for
the active sceners who are not afraid to exchange
their monitor radiation for some real sun!

* if the last sponsor-deals fall into place, we will
also give away some cool prizes!

* we will set up a network for spreading scene-stuff
and to make it easier for people to work on their
productions. this does NOT mean that we will accept
gaming! gaming is lame (with the exception of
bratwurst, slam tilt or larry 1 :)

* there is no internet-connection at the
partyplace, but since one of the main organizers is
born and raised just 15 minutes from the site, we
will have access to isdn to download demos or stuff
from your group-friends who could not make it
themselves.

* bigscreen for compos, demoshows and more!

* a very decent soundsystem (not run by deaf people)
and enough power for everyone.

* we've rented the gym at the local school for
access to plenty of showers.

* as a base rule, only people that over the age of
18 will be allowed to come. however! if you're
really cool but under 18, send us an email and
we'll consider to let you in anyway. :)

* norwegian law applies and neither we, nor the law
itself, allows for the use of drugs. we will allow
drinking, but this is a demoparty, not a beer-fest,
so the enjoyment of beer is primarily ment as a
social and enjoyable event and not as a way to get
unbelievably drunk and do silly things to
lamp-posts. :)

____________________________________________________how:


the partyplace is called "kroer samfunnshus", and is
located in ås.

to get in you will have to pay an entrance-fee of
course. we have expenses you know.

the fee is 150 NOK / 20 EUR for foreginers or if paid in
advance and 200 NOK / 25 EUR at the door. (currency-
conversion here if you need it)

the last date for paying in advance is the 20th of june,
a week before the party. if you haven't paid by then,
you will have to pay the extra 50 NOK at the door to
get in.

also; we would like to specify that putting up your tent
outside of the party- building and refusing to pay
because "i'm not inside, i don't need the power" will
not be permitted. the ticket-fee goes to rent both the
building and the space outside, aswell as to provide
technical stuff and other services. anyways; 150 NOK
is cheap - it's nothing. :)

if you know you are coming, but can't/don't want to pay
in advance, please register by mailing your details to
silmaril (silmaril@online.no).

make your payment using the following name, address and
account information. what you need may differ between
countries, but this should be enough in most cases.
if your bank still needs more information please contact
silmaril@online.no.

remember to include your name and email address in the
payment information-field. oh! please include your
nick/alias and group when paying/registering, for our
own record and so that we can add you to the list of
attendees on the website.

it's easy to get there - choose your mode of
transportation:

train from oslo: see www.trafikanten.no, use "Oslo
Sentralstasjon [tog]" as place of departure and "Ås
(tog)" as place of arrival.

train from sweden: get to oslo s. use "train from
oslo"-instructions above.

car from anywhere: find norway. find out where in
norway you are. use this cool website to create a
pleasant route for you to use.

airplane: get to oslo (taxi, airport-train, normal
train, airport bus - all leaving from oslo airport)
and then use the "train from oslo"-instructions above.

teleportation: please lock your coordinates to the
grassy field outside of the partyplace. if everyone
tries to beam directly into the party we could be
looking at quite a large mess.
