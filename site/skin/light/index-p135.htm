  SCH QPA - Demos on a building! ..274 clicks..
We have already learned that desperate coders can make a demo for anything that uses electricity, but perhaps a demo on a building's streetfront is still weird enough? Not for the university students who participated in the annual fun festival of the Budapest Technical University, the famous SCH QPA. They've wired all the room lights of the 19 story building of the Schönerz Dormitory into a control panel, and used it as a raster display device to play little animations and demos! Download the videos of the event.
