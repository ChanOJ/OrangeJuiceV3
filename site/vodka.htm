vodka / digital^saturne a.k.a vincent oneto has gone...

Vodka was the main organiser of the famous Saturne Party, the biggest
CP in France with the Volcanic Party. He was part of the French Scene
foundations, and his disappearance will leave a vacuum.

I met him a the end of the Saturne 5 and we kept good relations since,
thinking of a Saturne revival with the help of the LTP staff.

He was 26...

This page will stay in front of OJ for a while in homage to him.
