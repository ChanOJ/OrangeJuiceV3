Outline 2006 - multiplatform demoparty

Friday, June 2. - Monday, June 5.

Panoven, Zevenaar, The Netherlands
__/ __/

INVITATION
__ __________________ __


We are happy to invite you to Outline! Outline is a multiplatform
demoscene party that will take place in June 2006 in the east of
Holland.

The party will be a great opportunity to show your creative skills,
have a beer with your friends and ofcourse we will provide enough
space so you can use or show your hardware and software.

Outline used to be an Atari oriented event. This year we have
expanded our organising team and changed the date of the party, in
an effort to accomodate sceners who would traditionally visit other
events during Easter weekend.

__/ __/

PARTY LOCATION AND FACILITIES
__ __________________ __

The location for Outline 2006 is:

Recreatieoord "de Panoven"
Panovenweg 18
6905 DW Zevenaar
The Netherlands
http://www.panoven.com

Zevenaar is a small city in the east of Holland, only minutes from
the German border. Town centre is about 1500m from the party
location and the railway station is 800m.

"De Panoven" holiday resort is a well-preserved industrial heritage
estate dating from 1860. The kiln and associated buildings have
been transformed into a museum and a group accomodation.

You can find a number of affordable restaurants at walking distance
from the partyplace in Zevenaar city centre. We will also arrange a
pizza delivery service, and ofcourse you can buy cooled drinks for
reasonable prices at the partyplace.

The conference halls are equipped with suitable audio equipment and
beamer. There's a table and chair for every visitor, and some
relaxing couches as well (how many will depend slightly on the
number of visitors). Those who visited PMP will be happy to hear
there's a kitchen table, possibilities for a campfire, and, for the
first time since 1999, a pond for the PMP duckies. You can once
again put up a tent or caravan outside the party place if you prefer
that over the sleeping facilities provided by the organisation.

TICKETS
__ __________________ __


A ticket for the entire party (4 days and 3 nights) costs :
- Presales 45 EURO
- At the door 55 EURO

A single day ticket (valid for 24 hours excluding the sleeping area):
- Presales 12 EURO
- At the door 15 EURO


PLEASE NOTICE:

It is possible to rent private sleeping facilities for groups of 2
upto 10 individuals for a reasonable price. Contact Havoc for a price
quote. If you do not make such reservations, the organisers will
assign you to a sleeping place based on the date you payed your
ticket (those who pay first will be assigned to the more luxurious
facilities ofcourse).

There are no discounts possible on the entry fees except those
mentioned above. Presales tickets must be payed in advance.

We realize that these prices may be slightly higher than sceners got
used to in recent years. This is mainly because the party is held at a
commercial location, unlike previous parties which were usually in
schools, universities or clubhouses. We are convinced that the
facilities offered by such a location will compensate for the higher
fee.

We would also like to stress that even though the party location is
commercial, the organising team is working on a strictly non-profit
base. Any profit the party will generate will flow directly into the
competition prize fund, and any money left after the competitions
will be donated to a worthy cause. For example, after Outline 2005
we donated the leftover pennies to Outline 2006.

TRAVELLING
__ __________________ __

By plane: The preferred airport for travelling to this party is
Amsterdam/Schiphol. Flights from nearly every major airport on the
planet arrive there on a daily basis, and travelling from the airport
to the partyplace is easy because of the excellent railway
connections. A trainticket from Schiphol to the partyplace costs
16.50 Euro.

Other nearby airports include:

- Niederrhein
- Rotterdam
- Eindhoven
- Düsseldorf
- Münster/Osnabrück
- Dortmund

The organisers can provide you with information on how to get from any
of these airports to the partyplace. Contact us for details.

Please contact the organisers if you need any assistance regarding
flights to the party or extending your stay in the Netherlands.

http://www.lowcostairlines.nl/

By train: The railwaystation "Zevenaar" is located at 800m distance
from the partyplace. A map will be placed on our website soon.

By car:

From the East: Get on the highway from Oberhausen (Germany) to Arnhem
(A3/E35). Take the first exit (30) after the border, this is right
after you pass the former customs facility. Follow directions for
"Babberich" to the left. After passing two viaducts, you will reach a
T-junction. Follow the sign to the right for "Babberich". First you
will drive through the village of Babberich, then a roundabout
(straight on here). At the second roundabout, turn to the right so you
pass under the viaduct. You are now driving along a railroad- keep
doing that until you see the railwaystation. When you see the
railwaystation, turn left at the first opportunity (the second and
third are OK too). Drive straight ahead until you reach a T-junction
with the "Zuiderlaan". Turn left here, and follow the "Zuiderlaan"
(almost) until the end where you can turn to the right to the
"Panovenweg". Pass two streets to the left and follow the "Panovenweg"
to the right until you see "de Panoven". Welcome!

From the West: Get on the highway from Arnhem to Oberhausen (A12/E35).
Take the exit for Zevenaar (29). Turn to the right at the
trafficlights. Drive into Zevenaar and turn to the left at the first
trafficlight. Keep following the road you're on, passing 4 roundabouts
straight ahead, and then drive over the viaduct. At the roundabout
directly after the viaduct, turn to the left so you pass under the
viaduct. You are now driving along a railroad- keep doing that until
you see the railwaystation. When you see the railwaystation, turn left
at the first opportunity (the second and third are OK too). Drive
straight ahead until you reach the T-junction with the "Zuiderlaan".
Turn left here, and follow the "Zuiderlaan" (almost) until the end
where you can turn to the right to the "Panovenweg". Pass two streets
to the left and follow the "Panovenweg" to the right until you see
"de Panoven". Welcome!

PARTY RULES
__ __________________ __

First of all, we would like to say we have a lot of confidence in the
visitors we are inviting to this party. Some of the organisers have
been going to parties for more than a decade, and in all those years
the number of unacceptable incidents has been negligible.

However, we feel that setting a few clear guidelines will help
everyone to make the party even more enjoyable.

- The organisers are very tolerant, but there are limits. Please
treat other people and their property with respect. Illegal and/or
destructive behaviour will not be accepted and can lead to removal
from the party.

- No smoking in the main hall or sleeping area. Please step outside
or use the reserved spaces as appointed by the organisation. Weather
allowing, chairs and benches are allowed to be taken outside to the
terrace.

- Do not use electric waterboilers, microwaves, barbecues and other
devices that could possibly endanger the stability of computer
systems within the partyplace. Please contact the organisers before
setting up such equipment.

- We are only renting part of the space available at "de Panoven".
Please do not install systems or sleep in areas other than the main
halls or the sleeping areas.

Thank you for your participation in making this party a success.
