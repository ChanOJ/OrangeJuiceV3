What is this The idea for Oz96 was formed around mid-year 1995 on Bloodnet,
"demo-party" one of Australia's demoscene networks. It seemed that the
thing anyway? Australian demoscene has flourished enough to a point where a
party could be held with great attendance - this seems to
mirror the circumstances around NAID, North America's demoparty
(except that Oz96 will hopefully have better organisation :-).
Oz96 is not *truly* the first Australian demo party. There
have been some other small gatherings, such as Coven'95, but
Oz96 will be the first party involving the majority of the
Australian Scene.

Oz96 is a party for the demoscene, a gathering of all those
demofreaks out there as well as other demo-related occults such
as the underground music and art scenes. As such, in the
spirit of the scene, it is a friendly and most importantly
non-profit venture.

Demo - The maximum size of the complete, unarchived, executable demo
and ALL accompanying files must be less than 4096 kb. This
includes MIDI patches, etc.

- Any video Mode allowed from VGA to SVGA, up to a maximum of
640x480x256. But ANY SVGA demo MUST have VESA video bios
support! (Plus any other particular video card support is
optional)

- The maximum running length of the demo is 20 minutes.

- No pure animations or animations with players.

- The demo must not save anything to hard disk; assume 0 bytes
free.

- Only 1 entry per group is allowed.

- The demo must run without user interaction after any required
setup information is acquired from the user. If you do have a
Crystal Dreams 2-type interface after the demo is complete, it
is suggested that you include a commandline parameter to bypass
it. (This is to make it just that bit easier on the
organisers)

---------------------------------------------------------------

4kb Intro - The maximum size of the intro is *exactly* 4096 bytes.

- The intro cannot contain any sound or bitmapped graphics of
any description. If any textures are needed they must be
generated at run-time or initialisation.

- The maximum running length, including any initialisation
(i.e. from the time we hit Enter from DOS till the time the DOS
prompt comes up) is 5 minutes.

- Any video Mode allowed from VGA to SVGA, up to a maximum of
640x480x256. But ANY SVGA demo MUST have VESA video bios
support! (Plus any other particular video card support is
optional)

- The intro must not save anything to hard disk; assume 0 bytes
free.

- The intro must run without user interaction and must leave
the system in a stable state, in text mode, when finished.
No scummy savings of a few bytes by not quitting back to dos or
setting text mode, etc.

- The intro cannot give away any clues as to the identity of
the author.

- Code it in a high-level language if you dare =)

How does the All judging will be done at the party and whilst remote entries
voting work? may gain any placing, prizes will only awarded to those present
at the party site.

A person will be given a maximum of one prize per entry no
matter what the actual placing's are. (That's for those compos
that allow multiple entries - Demo and Music compos are max
1 entry per person)

Any prizes not awarded due to the prior rule will be given to
the next best entry.

You cannot enter something in more than one compo, that means
for example that any picture or music used in a demo cannot be
entered in the gfx/music compo as well.

Voting will be done by those present at the party place and
will be by a preferential voting system.

Voters will rank all the entries for a competition in the order
they think is best, and points will be allocated accordingly.

However, voters can also leave out entries altogether, and
zero points will be awarded - so placing an entry last is
still significant.

There will ALSO be judges, who's job will be to check that all
rules are followed, and that no cheating occurs. Judges will
also vote, and their vote will have double the value of
everyone else's! The judges will be announced at the party.

The organisers reserve the right to whatever they bloody well
want to with regards to the competition. :)
This includes final interpretation of the rules, so if you
have any queries, contact us beforehand.
