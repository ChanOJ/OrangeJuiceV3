:::[ SceneCON.2003 - where sceners meet ]:::
- party invitation v0.14.05.2003 -


Singular Crew and Inquisition is proud to invite you to the
SceneCON party of 2003!


.: WE DO APOLOGIZE :. . . . . . . . .

Salgótarján is giving place for the biggest Hungarian scifi meeting for
some couple of years. It is called Hungarocon. This year 5 more other
simposium joins the initiative, so is SceneCON. Anyone who will attend
our meeting, she/he will actually get in the middle of a big party that
affects the whole downtown.

(You can get more information about other sub-parties at the official
page of Hungarocon: http://hungarocon.scifi.hu
Prepare for some unexpected surprises! Special guests and programs
will do this weekend unforgettable...)


.: DATE :. . . . . . . . . .

Hungarocon is held 03-06.07.2003. but the main programs of SceneCON are
concentrated to 04-06.07. (friday-sunday).


.: HOW TO GET THERE :. . . . . . . .

Salgótarján can be found in north, north-east of Hungary, near to the
Slovakian border. It is easiy to reach by car or by train. Traveling
from Budapest you can use the highway M3 or the trunk-road 30. At the
city Hatvan you have to select the trunk road 21 which will lead to
the main square of Salgótarján. The train does the same way, and you
can get off near to the square too.
For more information you can use ELVIRA (http://www.elvira.hu, about
trains) or MENETRENDEK (http://www.menetrendek.hu, about coaches).


.: ENTRACE FEE :. . . . . . . . .

Buying the entry ticket, you are allowed to attend all the programmes of
Hungarocon. The arrangements of Scenecon - which will be held from 10am friday
to 6am sunday - can be entered by buying two one-day-tickets.

You can get some facility whith registering yourself, and ordering the
tickets in advance. It can be done at:

http://hungarocon.scifi.hu/reszvetel/online_regisztracio.html

.: QUARTERS :. . . . . . . . .

We think this question has not too much to talk about. If you are tired
eough, there will be a place for you to have a sleep in a corner. Who
needs some more comfort, there is possibility at the registration for
room in students hostel or hotel.


.: WHAT IS TO AVOID:. . . . . . . . .

As the localities are all public institutions, you have to search for places
fated for smoking. Avoid drugs and drinking too much alcohol: you have to
feel good somehow else. If you do anything lawless, you have to foot the
bill. If you damage anything you have to pay it.


.: WHAT IS WORTH TO BRING :. . . . . . . . .

Extension cords, bottle openers, UTP cord is never enough. If you are to use
some stream, hidroxil derivatives or local network, don't forget those things.
You might need a sleeping bag or anything else to have a sleep on. For voting
bring a pen too.


.: SHOWINGS :. . . . . . . . .

We want you not just feel good, but to spend your time even useful. That's
why we are organising presentations:

You might meet the Amiga-clone, Pegasos. The machine and the MorphOS operating
system will be shown, compatibility issues and software support will be
discussed and you can even hear about the Hungarian Pega community.

The "new" BeOS, the Zeta will show his beauty too. You can experience the
latest developments, interesting informations about Hungarian related
stuff and community. You will get a future-view of this platform.


.: COMPOS :. . . . . . . . .

The most important and most spectacular events of the party are of course the
compos. Here we will accept releases from outside of the party too. In this
case you have to be fast enough searching for an organiser as we can't promise
dowloading your work ten minutes before deadline. Search for an organiser

An author can compete with maximal 2 artworks in each competition. All of them
should have a file attached, named FILE_ID.DIZ covering party name (we help
you: SceneCON.2003), name of the author, group name (if there is) and category
where to compete. If you aren't present at the party, give us any possible way
to get in contact with you.

All the works will be preselected. They have to suit some minimal quality
condition. Forget 5-minutes-works, personalities, obscene and pornographic
releases. We won't show them. Virus affected files will be deleted without
notice.

Maximal showtime is 10 minutes for demos, 3 minutes for music compositions.
You have to know, that other platforms are even welcome, just let us know
if you want to show any Linux, BeOS, Atarai, Spectrum, Alcatel One Touch etc.
demo.


Compo machines are the following:

C64: C64c (new SID), 1541/II, IDE64 (HDD, CD)
Amiga: Amiga 1200, Blizzard 1260/50, 128Mb Fast RAM, Amiga OS 3.1
(PowerPC, 3D, faster 060, others can be there, but let us know in time)
PC#1: AMD Athlon XP 2200+, GeForce 4 Ti4200 64MB,
512Mb RAM, Windows XP, DirectX 9


Categories:

: 256 byte intro compo

Maximal 256 bytes, executable file.
Larger ones will be sent automatically to 4K category.
Disk access during showtime is forbidden, platforms compete together.

: 4k intro compo

Maximal 4096 bytes, executable file.
Larger ones will be sent automatically to 64K category.
Disk access during showtime is forbidden, platforms compete together.

: 64k intro compo

Maximal 65536 bytes, executable file.
Larger ones will be sent automatically to demo category.
Disk access during showtime is forbidden, platforms compete together.

: Demo compo (Amiga, PC)

Maximal 32 megabytes, should be executable.
Amiga and PC compete together, except if there are many competitors from
each platform.

: Retro demo compo (C64, Atari, Spectrum, etc.)

There will be C64 compomachine. If you want to compete on other retro
machine, bring your own too. At showtime we will tell the type of machine.

: C64 music compo

Artwork should be executable or in SID format (for playing SIDs we will use
Soci's SID plugin developed for IDE64 file manager). Optimize for new SID
as the compomachine will have this kind of sound!

: 4channel music compo

Maximal size is 1 Mb, accepted format is MOD.
Musics will be played with XMplay.

: Multichannel music compo

Maximal size is 3 Mbs, accepted formats are XM, IT, MOD, S3M.
Musics will be played with XMplay.

: MP3 music compo

Accepted formats are MP3, OGG.
Minimal bitrate is 128kbps.

: C64 GFX compo

Should be executable, can't scroll or animate. You have to enclose 3
stages too, wired art will be disqualified. You can help our work with
the source file of your editor, but it is not compulsory.

: Handdrawn GFX compo

Pictures done with handdrawn technics.
Maximal resolution is 800x600, should be a known file format.
(Picture can be larger, but it will be shown in this resultion. If you
don't want us to convert it, enclose a smaller version!)
You have to enclose 3 stages!

: Wild GFX compo

Any kind of technic is allowed, it can have scanned or retouched parts,
but the whole picture can't consist of a scan.
Maximal resolution is 800x600, should be a known file format.
(Picture can be larger, but it will be shown in this resultion. If you
don't want us to convert it, enclose a smaller version!)

: Raytrace GFX compo

Maximal resolution is 800x600, should be a known file format.
(Picture can be larger, but it will be shown in this resultion. If you
don't want us to convert it, enclose a smaller version!)
Minimal after-work is allowed (author's logo, lensflare, blur, volumetric
light, etc.) Enclose a wireframe rendered version!

: FUN compo

Wellknown competitions for teams, now a little bit anyhow else than before.
This time you won't need muscles to throw monitors around. You will get
exercrises which can be solved only by a real scener! ...but she/he has to
solve it for sure!

: Wild compo

Animations, video clips, and anything else that can't fit the rules before.

You might notice there won't be any "lemon" compo. As we accept jokes, quality
ones will compete in the wild compo. Other garvage-like stuff will be available
as SHIT.ZIP on the internet.

You can vote with a votesheet which you get with your entry to the place. If you
loose it, you can buy another entry ticket for voting.
