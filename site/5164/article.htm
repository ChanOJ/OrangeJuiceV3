Thanks for clicking. So, there's this shop: http://www.tomcatpolo.hu. Yeah, it's in Hungarian. We're selling our own t-shirts, mostly printed with funny and/or offensive printing. Our ad campaign involved placing some 8000 little stickers all around in Budapest, and some articles on selected online websites. This store is not really serving for the general public, and our shirts are available in limited numbers. We'd like to promote underground art in this shop. We're also selling amateur or semi-professional music CDs, video DVDs and demo collections, soon maybe vinyl EPs, and whatever comes, from all around the world. For this reason we'd be particularly interested in getting such material. If you have a musical album, a video colly, or a DVD, contact me! Even if it's home produced. We'd take some (4-5) copies to sell them as your commission agents. We're not making profit with this, because our goal is to saturate our shop's image, and besides, to promote your art in Hungary. We're selling on the price you set, and send you the money when the stock is gone. We're already affiliated with PNMF, Nosfe's and his friends' music label, Aural Planet, a Hungarian hiphop publisher called Gimmeshot, and hopefully we'll also sell the MindCandy DVD, the Assembly DVD and many more - negotiations are still underway. If you think you have something for this list, please mail me at tomcat@????, and tell us what do you have, what exactly it is, and how much would you like to sell it for. Everything is welcome! For party organizers: As I observed, printing party t-shirts in Hungary is much cheaper than in any EU country, even with shipping costs. We're printing on high quality, branded t-shirts (Gildan and Sol's) and use the same technologies as your local print service. Hence you might wish to consider ordering your shirts from us. The minimum order is 10 shirts, but for a real saving, 50 is recommended. That's all folks... :)
