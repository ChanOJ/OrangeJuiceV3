After some massive delay it finally went online:
The website for the Underground Conference 5 (uc5.25").

For those that never heard about UC before: The underground conference is the hardcore open-air party for the european demoscene, held in worms/germany from 12.7. to 15.7.2001.

To make sure it's 100% gamer/leecher/lamer-free, it's for invited sceners only.

The party is held inside a forest, far away from any civilization for maximum freedom. Still everything a scener needs to have fun is there: a huge partytent with enough space for all your equipment and an impressive soundsystem, crazy competitions, lots of power, stable network, perfect foodsupport, space for own sleeping-tents, fireplace .. even inet access!

So, if you think you are hard enough for 4 days of non-stop party action in the wildness, check http://uc5.untergrund.net/ now. Please keep in mind that you MUST register to this party, and that we have set a limit to max. 150 visitors to keep uc5.25" the small and friendly scener-meeting we are so in love with.
