WHAT IS IT ? 
 LIGHT VERSION 
 
 
September 20-21, 2003, Minsk, Belarus. These days we will have one of the largest ex-USSR demoscene events for the fourth time. The best sceners are going to compete in lots of compos, including demo and intro. The original idea of Millennium Demoparty was proposed by The Psycho Team in the year of 1999. The team itself is well-known by its projects, including Scenus Database, Scene e-paper, Demoscene Banners Collection, and some 200 releases. We took part in some 12 parties, our works often were award-winners. And of course, Millennium Demoparty was organized by the Psycho Team.

There is some kind of invisible gap between world and ex-USSR scene. We would like to overcome this gap during Millennium'2003 demoparty. Our scene cannot produce enough quality releases at the moment but we have a great progress with this. Usual users are receiving information about demoscene via mass media (newspapers, magazines, and TV). Some of our sceners are taking part in the events abroad, and they're even winning awards (at Assembly, for example). However, these cases are few. Pariticipation of world sceners in ex-USSR events will help to overcome the gap and rise the interest to demoscene events in our countries.

Millennium demoparty organizers had visited Symphony demoparty this year to gain some experience. We're going to do our best and hold the party at high level.

Please support our scene, send us modules, graphics, and demos. Everyone will appreciate your input.

Additional information can be found at our official site, http://mln.scenosaurus.org/en/. You can also contact us via e-mail: sergei@????, psych@????, cooler@????. Party rules are located here: http://mln.scenosaurus.org/en/rules1903.html

P.S. We're also glad to announce an Invitation compo. Write a Millennium Demoparty invitation intro of your own, and send it to us until September 10, 2003. It will compete among other invitations at the party, and winners will receive awards. If you cannot send the file directly to us at others@????, please send us a download URL, and we will publish it. If you need party logo and other information, we will send it to you.
