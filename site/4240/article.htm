Northern Dragons demo group celebrates its two-year anniversary. Established January 12,2002 in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada; we’ve competed at assembly twice, and are looking to compete again in 2003, as well as travel to the Pilgrimage demo event in the USA.

We’ve built and are building strong frameworks of technology, and are highly mobile – with a membership that spans Canada, and extends internationally. Our Internet enabled secured CVS Server allows all of us to work together collectively no matter where we are. But there is our problem as well.

We are really a bunch of coders, and a few musicians! So... we’ve learned that we need to extend our circle of membership to include: * Computer Artists – Pixelers, and Texture artists * Computer Modelers – People able to make 3d models * Webcoders. No – we won’t be forcing you to make a website.. instead we are interested in making html intro’s with some unique technology we’ve developed.

So, no mater where you are (we are open to everyone) – you’ve just finished reading this posting … so no doubt you are interested. So… check out, before it's too late!: http://www.northerndragons.ca
