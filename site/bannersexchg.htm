Our banners exchange system features views, clicks and click rate reports, expiration date, priority, border or not, target window and alt tag.

It displays between 10,000 and 20,000 banners a day on more than 100 websites all over the world.

Regarding to the settings it doesn't display anything else than the banner (no advertising for OJ nor for anything else) and the link to the corresponding website.

You have to display the banners exchange system on your pages to have your banners submissions being accepted.

The banners may be of one or both of the two following formats :
- 420 pixels width (60 pixels height max, 15kB max)
- 170 pixels width (30 pixels height max, 5kB max)

You can also force the border, the target or the text of the banner. For example, replace src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420.js"

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/noborder/banner420.js"
(to have no border around the banner)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/noborder/self/banner420.js"
(to display the site linked by the banner in the current window and to have no border around the banner)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/border/blank/banner420.js"
(to have a border around the banner and to display the site linked in a new window)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/border/notxt/banner420.js"
(to have a border around the banner and a no text below the banner)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/alttxt/banner420.js"
(to have the alt text displayed below the banner)
