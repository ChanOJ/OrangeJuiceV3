The Greenroom Mekka-Symposium Report!
exclusively for Orange Juice!

by: Vickey & Tomcat ^^ GREENROOM

Well... if you have never been to Mekka-Symposium neither this, nor another year, please forgive
me calling you a dumbass. This party simply kixx & roxx!! [This party is for
Vickey! - Vickey] Yes, my beautiful lady, the mascot of Greenroom is right here, making some
comments to the report. wqweeeeeeeeeeee12e (sorry she kissed me while typing :)

Now, let's see how we got to Fallingbostel, Germany to this ass-kicking (no, not licking)
annual Easter party. As usual, the Hungarian Amiga folks organized their annual bustrip,
this time for around twenty people, all from the Amiga scene, only we from Greenroom
presented the Hungarian PC scene at the spot. We encountered some HEAVY snowstorms on the
way. It is nice to travel on the motorway in beautiful sunshine, then suddenly running into
some deep fog, which is in fact a furious snow cloud. See Greenroom's video report for some
views of this: man, I've never seen so many snow in April, but to be honest, such weather is
rare even in January.

Let me not to report how our bus broke some 300 kilometers from Fallingbostel, which
resulted in a 60-80 km/h speed from then on, also all the way back, and besides forgive me
not writing about Vickey vomitting into the food bag. :) Finally we found the place, the
so-called Heidmarkhalle, which proved to be a perfect party building during the past years.
It was evening, the hall was already full with people, so we could hardly find a table in
the corner, where we immediately installed our Hungarian flag on the wall. Then came the
opening ceremony, we arrived right for its beginning. As always, the organizers gave us
great fun, last year they performed live singing, this year it was like the opening ceremony
of the Olympics, with the old C-64 game "Summer Games" shown on the bigscreen, and a true
olympic fire effect, of course. :) Later all the information texts displayed on the
bigscreen featured some pictures from Summer Games, but as the weather turned worse every
hour, they changed it to screenshots from "Winter Games" instead. :)

Yes, the weather was horrible. There was no sign of spring outside: snow changed with rain
in every hour, and there was bone-freezing cold. So we had to skip the so popular outdoors
programmes like barbecue making and so; however, there were some hardcore Finnish guys who
built a campfire and kept it burning even during the rain. On Saturday there was some little
sunshine, just enough to enable the organizers to hold the hard disk throwing competition.
The Hungarian ambassador on this event was Vickey, who successfully sent a poor 120 MB one to
the sky, winning a box of CDs. Congrats, Vickey! :) [You said I should imagine throwing it
to D-Lee-s head! - Vickey] Meanwhile there was some live act on the stage by the music
band "Welle Erdball", who are making music with SIDStation synthesizers, including some
remakes of old Commodore 64 tunes. All I can say that this German band rocks, and please
consider buying their CDs, they deserve it!

Party on, party on. Compos coming up! Well, unfortunately there were not as much and good
Hungarian releases as in last year - remember Heaven Seven? - but however, we had some nice
stuff. This year it was the Polish scene's time, they contributed to almost every compo, and
they took some nice prizes too. Unfortunately, not much people voted for the compos, and
many voted randomly, so most of the compos, mainly the graphic compos have a quite unfair
result. Let me not to say anything more about this, since I also contributed, but take a
look at the results file, then check the entries. You'll see what I mean.

Bigfoot of Breeze, the C-64 coder started making a brand new demo on Saturday, this became
Wildfire II, which you could see during the compos (unfortunately hanged). He had the idea
to put "some dancing chick" in it. Fortunately we brought our own dancing chick. There was
a Second Reality show early on Saturday, during which Vickey performed some dance
improvisation on the stage, which had been filmed for the demo, and caused some major
applause. [Thanks guys! I love to dance!! - Vickey]

During Saturday, despite of the bad winter weather, many people went outside to booze and
talk. At least a thousand gallons of alcohol had been wasted, so the nearby supermarket had
been drown dry. :) Watch the Greenroom video report to see some of the nerds acting outside:
for example, Xhale / Miasmah eating a chocolate along with the paper, PS / Calodox drawing
a Calodox logo which doesn't look like anything like a Calodox logo, XXX / Haujobb boozing
(of course :), the Hungarian Amiga freaks sharing joints with the Polish dudes, and so on.
There was a nice quiz show on the stage titled "Be a Scenionare", which was in fact a scene
version of "Be a Millionaire", with questions about the scene, like: "How many channels does
a 4 channel MOD have?", "Why does XXX / Haujobb always booze?" and so on. Well, these were
only the entry questions, later there were harder ones for the prize of one million euro. (As
a matter of fact, the entire thing seemed fake, but however, it was funny. :))

The music compos had been held on Saturday evening, where the Hungarian musics, mostly
Nagz / Greenroom's ones had great success among the audience. A smaller bunch of dancing
people gathered at the stage, later also on the stage, waving Polish, Norwegian and
Hungarian flags. Later, when they all turned dead drunk, they waved each others' flags
randomly, so no one could tell who is from which country. :) I hereby apologize the
organizers for stealing the mop from the shower room, but we needed some pole for our flag!

Let's stop here for a second, we have something to explain... Some of those who met the
Hungarian flag usually saw that in the hands of a stickman-like little prick, who is called
Voyager. He offended some people with this flag, and we have to apologize them. In fact we
don't really like that guy, and hopefully he will not come with us next year. Sometimes he was
acting like if he were doing some heavy drugs.
First he was waving the flag on the stage so widely that the security had to remove him
before he hits someone. (Meanwhile the Polish guys were not removed, although they also
waved their flag. Voyager was the only one who didn't understand what is the problem with him.)
Some hours later we were walking outside with Vickey, when the gate of the hall blasted open,
and we saw Voyager rushing inside. He looked at me and shouted: "Hilfe! Hilfe!" Now we had problems with understanding why the hell does he yell at us in German. Suddenly the gate had
been kicked open once more, and a rather angry and rather huge dude rushed after Voyager,
certainly with the will to break his bones. He was followed with another guy, carrying the
Hungarian flag, while building a rather surprised face. I asked him about where he is going
with my flag.

- That little guy threw it away outside - he replied.

Well, it came to light that this Voyager prick somehow hit the big guy with the flag on the
head. Later he himself reported that "that moron wanted to kill me, this is why I hit him
twice". The others who saw that reported that once he hit the guy with the flag accidentally, then when he turned angry, hit him once more, now by will. There were some more episodes
during the party when Voyager had almost been bet up, so I apologize those who he offended in
the name of the entire Hungarian expedition group.

Let's go on. Next day there was a surprise act on the stage. The organizers announced some
live didgeridoo chillout music, accompanied by a 808, and asked everyone having any instrument
to come up to the stage and join the show! In fact not much instruments had been found, but
soon our lovely lady, Vickey appeared on the stage dancing. As she is a professional
dancer, she really tuned up the show. Within a short time many guys were up on the stage,
dancing pogo in the background. Again Vickey had great success, and suddenly the number of her
fans greatly increased. :) Especially XXX / Haujobb loved her, but unfortunately she did not
join Haujobb. ;)

Yes, there were great demos and intros in the main competitions, and I can't tell how much
I loved those. My favourite PC demos were the winner Kolor demo "Le Petit Prince", and the
Polish demo titled "Optic" by Addict. The Black Lotus again showed how to code on the Amiga,
which meant them the first prize in the Amiga demo compo. Unfortunately the compos were
largely delayed, so I almost fell asleep during them. I would like to send a message here to
the C-64 group Padua. Guys, I see you can code nice things, but WHY do you think that a demo
should last HALF AN HOUR ?!! I love C64 and SID and so, but after a certain length it becames
awfully boring...

The party ended, as usual, with the prizegiving and closing ceremony. Unfortunately for us,
there was only one Hungarian winner, Crimson Jihad in the wild competition, with a PC
bashing video, but surprisingly it was really not that bad as such videos usually are. Last
year we had better results. But anyway, I really enjoyed this party, just as last year, and
I certainly will be present next year too! Now some quick greets to those I've met and had
fun with: Netpoet, A-Move, Xenon / Park, Muhmac, Mindflower and the others / Freestyle,
Yes, Chandra / Orange Juice, Hellfire, Acryl, XXX, Visualice and all the others / Haujobb,
Steeler, Tarzeau, PS & Fred / Calodox, Xhale and all the Norwegians, Virne / Coma, Neuroup
and the rest of Addict, Pyro, and of course to the organizers and everyone I forgot. Great
party! Come to FLaG 2001, or the doodle from tmk's notebook will turn rather angry! :)
