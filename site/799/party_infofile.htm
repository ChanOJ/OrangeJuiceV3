Introduction
------------

What are demo parties? Traditionally they are a collection of computer
enthusiasts, creating and competing with demos (realtime displays of
special effects, graphics and music). The Alternative Party attempts to
bring all of this to a wider audience of artists and people interested in
digital media and experimentation. The demoscene, as a flourishing
sub-culture, deserves to receive even more attention than it has so far.
We attempt to question the boundaries of this area. To push it into new
areas.

This is the sixth Alternative Party. It is celebrating (or parodying) the
notions of corporate image and identity. The disappearance of the
individual behind brand names and corporate policy. Our lives are often
bundled into jobs where we no longer exist as human beings. Becoming part
of an concrete, unliving being, which controls our output, if not always
our thought. Where free expression is discouraged and tinkering with
personal goals and projects is considered a sin. How can someone want to
create something outside their work? We are asking that people are given
the right to offer opinions and views. That they are not merely cogs in
machinery. That they have personality beyond a brand name.

The Alternative Party has always attempted to be a force for change in the
demoscene. Not all find this an easy proposal, but what is a culture
without change? Without new ideas (even, occassionally, bad ones)? It
gathers together a varying bunch of computer freaks, geeks and artists on
machines of many different shapes and kinds. All working to show their
skills, their ideas and their individuality to the world.

The Alternative Party is crazy and weird, but it is also extremely fun.

Competitions
------------

General Rules

* All entries must be submitted via our intranet - where applicable.
* Use descriptive filenames.
* At least one of the authors of the entry must be present at the party.
* There may be pre-screenings during the party. Entries can be disqualified
because of their content or, for example, because there is no machine the
entry can run on. The organisers have ultimate power.
* Any machine running a competition entry in the competition may not be
connected to the Internet. The entries may contain features that use the
net, but they must work without the net, too.


Alternative Demo Competition

* You know what a demo is, right? If not, see Wikipedia's description
(http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demo#In_Computers).
* The purpose of this competition is to bring out new ideas in demos, whatever
the platform and method. A field for creativity.
* Maximum length is 8 minutes, or until we get bored.

Dynamic Demos

* We wish to continue to push this idea from Alt V as we believe it to be one
worth exploring.
* Dynamic demos will be shown twice as part of the main demo competition and
will compete with the other demos there.
* In addition, they will be ranked in their own series and the best will
receive their own good prizes (TBA). Note that this means that a good
dynamic demo can win two prizes - both in the normal category as well as the
dynamic one.
* The demo must be noticeably different every time it is run. It can use
randomisation or evolution or any other technique to accomplish this. Your
mind is the limit.
* How exactly it should change is left for the participants to decide, though
be warned that watching the same demo with only slight randomisation can be
boring.
* Untraditionally for demos, dynamic demos may write to the disk for the
purpose of evolution.


Telephone Queue Music Competition

* Music to be played in a telephone queue or f.ex. a lift.
* Maximum playing time is 45 seconds.
* The entry should be in MP3 or OGG format.


Logo Competition

* Design a logo for Alternative Party.
* Allowed file formats: SVG, PS, PDF, GIF, PNG, JPEG, TIFF


Music Video Competition

* Maximum length is 5 minutes or until we get bored.
* The video must be delivered as an MPEG file.
* The music or the video must not have been previously released or distributed.
* The production must state the names of the artist and the song at the
beginning of the video.


Presentation Competition

* You have ten minutes to give the audience a presentation using a microphone
and a computer running either Microsoft PowerPoint or OpenOffice.org Impress.
* A maximum of two people are allowed to participate in the presentation
* The presentation file (one .ppt or .sxi file) must be submitted to
organisers beforehand. You may not use other files.
* You may use whatever you can embed into the file - macros, pictures, music -
you name it.


Most Obscure Computer Competition

* As usual we will pick the most obscure, strange or rare machine or piece of
hardware we can find.


More competitions to be announced.
